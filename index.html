How it works:
Set 1: Players split randomly and evenly across available courts. Each court runs a round robin.
Set 2 & 3: Top 4 from each court → Court 1. Bottom 4 from each court → Court 2.
Scoring: Match max = 15 pts. Each player receives half their team's score.
Point Calculation Method: 15 points sudden death, no deuce.
